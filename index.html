01 · About me

AI Animation · Motion Graphics Artist

Hi, I'm Michael Angelo

I’m an animation and motion graphics artist who builds AI into every frame. I turn scripts into 3D animated ad stories with characters brought to life in Higgsfield, Kling AI, and Seedance, design kinetic type and motion graphics in After Effects, and draw complex ideas into simple whiteboard animations. AI voiceovers from ElevenLabs and music from Suno give every scene its timing, and I finish each piece in Premiere Pro and CapCut. The result is animated ads that stop the scroll, made faster without cutting corners on quality. I’m highly organized, deliver on tight deadlines, and work well with teams in fast-paced production.

My animation style
Characters with real emotion, motion that always has a reason, and hooks that land in 3 seconds.
What I animate
3D animated story ads, animated music ads, motion graphics, kinetic type, and whiteboard explainers.
What I care about
Story first. Every movement has to earn its place on the timeline.
Delivered as
9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 cuts, on-brand and on deadline.
